## Objective

See the title and the audit sections above. State the goal in one sentence in
your own words before making any change.

## Scope

Review the completed analytics feature for privacy and security. Verification
task.

## Scope
1. Audit what is actually stored, not what the design intended.
2. Confirm no personal data, no fingerprinting, and no cross-session linkage.
3. Confirm the new endpoints are abuse-resistant and correctly authorized.
4. Confirm the free-tier assumptions still hold with real numbers.
5. Write the findings under `docs/` and update the roadmap audit trail.

## Non-goals
Do not implement fixes silently. Report defects as findings with evidence; fix
only a security defect, and say explicitly that you did and why. Do not change
behaviour to make a finding disappear.

## Implementation requirements
Base every claim on the code and on observed data. Quote the actual storage
shape. If you cannot prove anonymity, say so rather than asserting it.

## Required tests

## Stop conditions

Stop and report BLOCKED rather than improvising if:

- The task turns out to contradict the approved audit.
- The change turns out to require a production deployment to validate.
