## Non-goals
No admin UI. No aggregation queries beyond what the design requires. No
tracking of individuals. No third-party analytics service. No departure from the
5.1 design, and no scope invented here. If the design is ambiguous, stop and
report BLOCKED rather than choosing.

## Implementation requirements
The collection endpoint must be abuse-resistant by design, not by retrofit:
follow the Phase 4 lessons, which now exist in the code. Events must be
anonymous by construction, and the privacy boundaries from 5.1 must be enforced
in code and provable by test. Storage growth must be bounded or explicitly
accepted in writing.

## Required tests
Cover, at minimum: events are accepted and stored; nothing identifying is
stored; the abuse protections from Phase 4 are present on the new endpoint;
volume is bounded as designed; and the endpoint is not reachable in a way that
exceeds the free-tier assumptions.
